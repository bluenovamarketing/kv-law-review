Gabrielle’s current firm biography describes a practice focused on insurance-fraud matters with a concentration in health care fraud. Her work includes the analysis of medical records, claim data, insurance laws and regulations.

She has represented insurance carriers as well as insured individuals and businesses. Her published experience spans auto, commercial liability and premises-liability matters, including disputes involving fraud, coverage and liability issues.

Matters involving health care claims can require the disciplined comparison of medical records, billing information, policy terms and participant relationships. Gabrielle’s background connects that factual work to the litigation and coverage questions a matter presents.
