Complex insurance claims

Danyelle’s current firm biography describes more than two decades in practice and extensive representation of insurance carriers and their insureds. Her insurance work includes auto-theft matters, examinations under oath, first- and third-party coverage, no-fault/PIP disputes, personal-injury litigation, premises liability and uninsured- and underinsured-motorist coverage.

She has assisted carriers with claim investigations and worked on sophisticated insurance-fraud schemes. That experience connects detailed claim development to the coverage and litigation questions the record may present.

Earlier in practice, Danyelle worked in medical-malpractice defense and participated in all stages of litigation for physicians, nurses and hospitals. Her judicial clerkships and service as a court-appointed civil mediator add further perspective on procedure and dispute resolution.

Professional background

Litigation informed by
judicial service.

Danyelle served as a judicial law clerk to the Honorable Jerome St. John, J.A.D., in the Appellate Division of the Superior Court of New Jersey.

Before that appointment, she clerked for the Honorable Ronald S. Reisner, J.S.C., in the Criminal Division of the Superior Court in Monmouth County and served as a court-appointed civil mediator.
