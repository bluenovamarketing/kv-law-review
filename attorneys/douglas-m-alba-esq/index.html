Douglas M. Alba, Esq.

Douglas M. Alba investigates and litigates civil insurance-fraud matters and handles disputes involving personal injury protection benefits and uninsured-motorist benefits. His approach is informed by public service and extensive private-practice work for insurance carriers.

Douglas began his litigation career as a Deputy Attorney General in the New Jersey Division of Law. He litigated civil fraud cases for the Medicaid program and served as counsel to public bodies including the New Jersey Spinal Cord Injury Commission, the Board of Nursing Home Administrators and the Department of Human Services’ HIPAA Compliance Committee.

In private practice, he has represented insurance carriers in civil matters arising under the New Jersey Insurance Fraud Prevention Act. His work requires careful analysis of claim files, provider relationships, testimony and the statutory or regulatory issues that shape a dispute.

At Kennedy Vuernick, Douglas continues to investigate and litigate civil insurance-fraud matters. His published firm biography also identifies work involving personal injury protection benefits and uninsured-motorist benefits litigation.

Professional background

Douglas’s public-sector work involved civil Medicaid fraud litigation and counsel roles for state bodies. His private-practice work has centered on insurer representation, civil fraud claims and insurance-benefit disputes.

The result is a practice grounded in both government process and the operational detail of complex claim records.
