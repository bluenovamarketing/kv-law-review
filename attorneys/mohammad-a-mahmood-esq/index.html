Mohammad A. Mahmood, Esq.

Mohammad A. Mahmood is a former Deputy Attorney General in the New Jersey Division of Criminal Justice. His public-service record includes complex criminal cases involving auto-theft trafficking networks, racketeering enterprises and expert scientific evidence.

As a Deputy Attorney General, Mohammad represented the State of New Jersey in complex criminal cases. His current firm biography identifies matters ranging from auto-theft trafficking networks to racketeering enterprises.

His public-service record includes the Attorney General’s Award for Excellence in Litigation following a multi-week Frye hearing in which he directed and cross-examined expert witnesses in scientific disciplines including biostatistics.

The biography also reports the Division of Criminal Justice Director’s Award for Excellence in Investigation for the prosecution of Genovese crime-family members as part of a racketeering indictment. These details are presented as professional background, not as promises about any future matter.

Professional background

Scientific training,
legal analysis and advocacy.

Mohammad earned undergraduate degrees in chemistry and philosophy before attending Rutgers School of Law. That combined training is relevant to a record involving technical evidence, expert methodology and competing factual theories.

At Rutgers, he graduated magna cum laude and served as lead state constitutional law editor of the Rutgers Law Journal.
