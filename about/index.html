The starting point

A focused anti-fraud practice

The firm behind
the investigation.

Fraud matters rarely arrive with a complete or straightforward record. Relevant facts may be spread across contracts, claims data, medical records, financial records, correspondence and witness accounts. Kennedy Vuernick approaches that complexity by identifying the relevant record, testing competing explanations and developing a strategy around what the evidence supports.

The firm’s work may begin before litigation, continue through filing and discovery, or take the form of an independent investigation or monitoring assignment. When a matter requires specialized investigative or technical support, the firm works with trusted investigative partners and other qualified professionals to help develop the record.

The approach

Understand. Follow.
Build deliberately.

Understand the record. Begin with the documents, data, history and people connected to the matter.

Follow the evidence. Test assumptions, identify inconsistencies and determine what the available facts support.

Build deliberately. Shape the legal or investigative strategy around the record rather than forcing the record to fit a predetermined theory.
