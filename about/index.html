The starting point

Evidence before assumption

Complex matters require
disciplined analysis.

Fraud can be difficult to detect and even harder to prove. Kennedy Vuernick approaches these matters by examining the record closely—documents, data, timelines and other available evidence—before developing a strategy grounded in the facts.

The firm does not shy away from detail. Its work reflects careful investigation, deliberate analysis and the understanding that complex fraud matters must be built fact by fact.

The approach

Understand. Follow.
Build deliberately.

Understand the record. Begin with the documents, data, history and people connected to the matter.

Follow the evidence. Test assumptions, identify inconsistencies and determine what the available facts support.

Build deliberately. Shape the legal or investigative strategy around the record rather than forcing the record to fit a predetermined theory.
